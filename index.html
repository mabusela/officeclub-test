Office Club Sandton is a premier office destination located at 28 saddle drive, Woodmead, Sandton, on the landmark corner of N1 & R55. This office facility is a 3-storey building that has a gross area of 7802 M2, and has been divided into 11 office suites.

This class A building has been recently renovated with world class finishes and features, updated into sophisticated open, modern office suites with private kitchens and restrooms, as well as updated HVAC systems and convenient parking.

Office Club Sandton is located with excellent visibility along N1 & R55 with a traffic count of 250 000 VPD. This offers an unbeatable branding opportunity for your business through signage.
